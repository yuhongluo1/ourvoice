关于 Our VoiceABOUT

学生自己发起、为学生而做。你对学校有想法、建议或意见，却不知道跟谁说——我们接住这些声音，带到学校，把结果公开。 A program made by students, for the students. If you have suggestions, ideas or concerns about the school and no one to tell, we take them to the school and publish what happens.

🎯 我们做什么 / What we do

把学生对学校的声音带到学校，并把结果公开在回执墙；同时帮社团登广告、留存成果。 We carry student voices to the school and publish the outcome on the Receipt Wall. We also help clubs post ads and keep their achievements on record.

📮 怎么找到我们 / How to reach us

发邮件到 ourvoice@hd.kaiwenacademy.cn；已经解决的事都在回执墙。 Email us — what has already been resolved is on the Receipt Wall.
