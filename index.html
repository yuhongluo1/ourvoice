关于 Our VoiceABOUT

一个由学生自己发起、自己运营的组织。你可以把我们理解成校园里的"广告公司"——但我们服务的客户，是整个学生社区。 A student-run organization. Think of us as the campus "ad agency" — except our client is the whole student community.

🎯 我们做什么 / What we do

不办活动（那是学生会和社团的事）。我们做三件事：把学生的声音带到学校并公开结果（反馈线）；让社团、活动与成果被全校看见（传播线）；让消息找到人、人找到人（连接）。 We do not run events (that is for the Student Council and clubs). We do three things: bring student voices to the school and publish outcomes (feedback); make clubs, events and achievements visible school-wide (media); help information find people, and people find people (connection).

🏛 和学生会的关系 / Student Council

学生会是策展方——他们办活动、给资源；我们是做传播的——让活动找到人、让成果被记住。两件事不重叠，经常合作。 The Student Council curates — they run events and provide resources. We spread the word — helping events find people and achievements get remembered. No overlap; frequent collaboration.

🕰 我们做过什么 / Our record

办过 Leadership Face-to-Face 论坛（2025.12，学生直接对话校领导）、组织过全校性问题汇总反馈，也推动了一批切实的校园改变——从教室投影、报告厅座椅，到卫生间改造与无障碍卫生间重新开放。全部记录在回执墙 / the Receipt Wall。 We ran the Leadership Face-to-Face Forum (Dec 2025 — students face-to-face with school leaders), organized school-wide issue reports, and helped push real campus changes: from projectors and lecture-hall seats to restroom renovations and the reopening of the accessible restroom. All recorded on the Receipt Wall.
