关于 Our VoiceABOUT

学生自己发起、自己运营。可以理解成校园里的"广告公司"——客户是整个学生社区。 Student-run. Think of us as the campus "ad agency" — our client is the whole student community.

🎯 我们做什么 / What we do

活动由社团和学生会办；我们做传播和记录。自己主办的只有一场：Leadership Face-to-Face 论坛。 Clubs and the Student Council run events; we do publicity and records. The one event we run ourselves is the Leadership Face-to-Face forum.

🏛 和学生会的关系 / Student Council

学生会办活动、给资源；我们做传播，让活动找到人、让成果被记住。经常合作。 The Student Council runs events and provides resources; we spread the word. Frequent collaborators.

🕰 我们做过什么 / Our record

主办过 Leadership Face-to-Face 论坛（2025.12），推动过一批校园改变：投影仪、报告厅座椅、卫生间改造、无障碍卫生间重新开放。全部记在回执墙 / the Receipt Wall。 We ran the Leadership Face-to-Face forum (Dec 2025) and helped push real campus changes: projectors, lecture-hall seats, restroom renovations, the accessible restroom reopening. All on the Receipt Wall.
